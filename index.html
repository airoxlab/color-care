California requires a contractor's licence for any painting job over $500 in labour and materials. Plenty of people painting Sacramento homes do not hold one — and if an uninsured painter is hurt in your house, that can become your problem.

  • Active C-33 licence — the classification specific to painting and decorating.
  • $25,000 contractor's bond with Nationwide Mutual, on file with the state.
  • Workers' compensation insurance carried for our crew.
  • The licence number is on our yard sign — we would rather you checked it.
